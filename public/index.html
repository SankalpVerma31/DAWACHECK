What happens inside

  1. 1. Normalise“DOLO 650 TAB 15'S” is split into brand, strength, form, pack size and price.
  2. 2. Fuzzy matchTrigram + edit-distance search finds candidate salts, even with OCR typos.
  3. 3. Rules checkStrength and dosage form must agree, or the match is downgraded.
  4. 4. Price verdictPer-unit price vs NPPA ceiling + 12% GST. Above it = illegal.
  5. 5. Show confidenceEvery line carries a score. We never pretend to be sure.

    Why this matters

    39%

    of India's total health spending still comes straight out of people's pockets (NHA 2021–22 estimates), and medicines are the single largest slice.

    900+

    essential drug formulations have a legally binding ceiling price under the DPCO. Charging more is an offence — but almost no patient can check.

    18,000+

    Jan Aushadhi Kendras sell the identical molecule for 50–80% less. Most people standing at a chemist counter have never been told.

    DawaCheck is an informational tool built on public data. Prices in this demo are a seed dataset modelled on published NPPA and PMBI lists and may be out of date. Always verify against the official NPPA ceiling price list before filing a formal complaint. This is not legal or medical advice.
